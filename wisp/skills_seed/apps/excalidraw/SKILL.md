# excalidraw

## Teaching

### Where things live
- The toolbar sits across the top: lock, hand, selection, shapes, arrow, line, pen, text, image, eraser. The menu is at the top left and the properties panel appears on the left when something is selected.

### First steps
- Tool keys: `V` selection, `R` rectangle, `D` diamond, `O` ellipse, `A` arrow, `L` line, `P` pencil, `T` text.
- The menu at the top left has Open, Save to, Export image and Clear canvas.
- Double click the canvas to type a text label.

### Pitfalls
- Clear canvas removes everything; the drawing lives in the browser unless exported or shared.
