# shotcut

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Top: a toolbar with Open File, Save, Undo, Redo and panel toggles. Centre: the player. Bottom: the timeline.
- Panels such as Playlist, Filters and Export are toggled from the toolbar or the View menu.

### First steps
- `Ctrl+O` opens a file. `Space` plays and pauses. `S` splits the clip at the playhead.
- `Ctrl+Z` undoes. Click Export in the toolbar, choose a preset, then Export File.

### Linux pitfalls
- `Ctrl+S` saves the project only; the finished video exists only after Export File runs.
