# google-maps

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Top left: the search box with a Directions button beside it. Bottom left: the Layers tile. The zoom buttons are at the bottom right.
- A place card opens on the left with Save, Share and Directions.

### First steps
- Directions asks for a start and a destination, then shows travel modes.
- Layers at the bottom left switches to satellite or terrain.
- On a place card, Save adds it to a list such as Favorites.

### Pitfalls
- Location sharing and Timeline are private; do not read saved addresses aloud.
