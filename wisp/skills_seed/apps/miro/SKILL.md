# miro

## Teaching

### Where things live
- The creation toolbar runs down the left of a board: select, templates, text, sticky note, shapes, connectors, pen, frames. Share is at the top right.
- The zoom control is at the bottom.

### First steps
- Click the sticky note icon, then click the board to place it.
- The Frames tool outlines a region to group content.
- Share opens the invite dialog and the access level.

### Pitfalls
- Board access levels decide who can edit; check the Share dialog before telling anyone to invite.
