# inkscape

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left: the toolbox. Top: the menu bar and a tool controls bar that changes with the tool. Right: dockable dialogs. Bottom: the colour palette and status bar.
- Fill and Stroke, Layers and Objects, and Align and Distribute are under the Object and Layer menus.

### First steps
- `S` selects, `R` draws a rectangle, `E` an ellipse, `B` a pen path, `T` text, `N` edits nodes.
- `Ctrl+Shift+F` opens Fill and Stroke. `Ctrl+D` duplicates, `Ctrl+G` groups, `Ctrl+Shift+G` ungroups.
- `Ctrl+Shift+E` opens Export. `5` zooms to fit the page.

### Linux pitfalls
- `Ctrl+S` saves Inkscape SVG; a PNG or PDF for sharing comes from Export or Save a Copy, not from Save.
