# notion

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left: the sidebar with workspace, search and page tree. Top right of a page: Share and the more menu. A page is made of blocks.
- The New page button sits at the bottom of the sidebar or beside Teamspaces.

### First steps
- Type `/` on an empty line to open the block menu and pick a block type.
- `Ctrl+P` opens search to jump to a page.
- Share at the top right sets who can view or edit.
- Drag the handle left of a block to move it.

### Pitfalls
- Databases have several views; the view tabs sit above the table.
