# linkedin

## Teaching

### Where things live
- The top bar has the search box, then Home, My Network, Jobs, Messaging and Notifications. Me opens a menu with View Profile and Settings. Start a post is the box at the top of the feed.

### First steps
- Jobs in the top bar opens job search with filters.
- Me, then View Profile, then the pencil icons edit sections.
- Start a post at the top of the feed opens the composer.

### Pitfalls
- Messages and connection lists are private; do not read them aloud.
