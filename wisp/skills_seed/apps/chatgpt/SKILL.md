# chatgpt

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left sidebar: New chat, Search chats, then your chat history. The message box is at the bottom; the model picker is at the top left. Your account menu with Settings is at the bottom left.

### First steps
- New chat at the top of the sidebar starts a fresh conversation.
- Search chats in the sidebar finds older ones.
- The model name at the top of the page opens the picker.

### Pitfalls
- Chat history is personal; do not read it aloud.
