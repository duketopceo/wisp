# figma

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left: Layers and Assets. Middle: the canvas with the toolbar along the top or bottom. Right: the Design panel, plus Prototype.
- Share is at the top right.

### First steps
- Tool keys: `V` move, `F` frame, `R` rectangle, `O` ellipse, `L` line, `T` text.
- The Design panel on the right sets size, fill and stroke for the selection.
- Share, then set who can view or edit and copy the link.

### Pitfalls
- Dev Mode changes the right panel to inspect code; switch it off to edit again.
