# bitwarden

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left: Vaults, Send, Tools and Reports, Settings. The search box is at the top, and the New button adds an item. In the desktop app the vault list is in the middle and the item details on the right.

### First steps
- New, then Item, adds a login; fill in name, username and password.
- Tools, then Generator, makes a password.
- The search box filters items by name or username.

### Pitfalls
- Never read usernames, passwords, notes or codes aloud; point at the field and let the user look.
