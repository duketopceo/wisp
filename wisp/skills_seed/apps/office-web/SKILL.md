# office-web

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Top left: the app launcher grid. Left: the navigation rail with Create, My content and Apps. In OneDrive, the command bar above the file list has New, Upload and Share.
- The editors show a ribbon with Home, Insert and other tabs.

### First steps
- The app launcher grid opens Word, Excel and PowerPoint.
- Upload sits in the OneDrive command bar; choose Files or Folder.
- Select a file, then Share in the command bar.
- Editors save automatically; the title area shows the save state.

### Pitfalls
- OneDrive for work and personal accounts look alike but are different hosts and accounts.
