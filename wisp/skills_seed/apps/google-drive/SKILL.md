# google-drive

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Top left: the New button. Left: My Drive, Shared with me, Recent, Starred and Trash. Top: the search box.
- The right side details panel shows sharing and activity for the selected item.

### First steps
- New, then File upload or Folder upload.
- Right click an item and pick Share to add people or change link access.
- The search box takes filters such as type and owner from its options button.
- Deleted items go to Trash and can be restored from there.

### Pitfalls
- Shared drives and My Drive are separate trees with different permissions; check which one is open.
