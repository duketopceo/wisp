# canva

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Home has a Create a design button and a template search. In the editor the left panel has Design, Elements, Text, Brand, Uploads and more.
- Share is at the top right of the editor.

### First steps
- Create a design on Home, then pick a size or a template.
- The Text tab in the left panel adds a heading or body text box.
- Share, then Download, then pick the file type.

### Pitfalls
- Some templates and elements are paid and carry a crown icon; point out the free alternatives.
