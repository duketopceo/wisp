# microsoft-teams

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Far left: the app bar with Activity, Chat, Teams, Calendar and Calls. Top: the search box. In a meeting: the toolbar at the top with Mic, Camera and Share.
- Settings and more is the three dots at the top right.

### First steps
- The new chat button above the chat list starts a conversation.
- Calendar lists meetings; open one and press Join.
- The Share button in the meeting toolbar picks a screen or window.
- `Ctrl+E` focuses the search box.

### Pitfalls
- Work and personal accounts use different sites; the profile picture at the top right shows which one is signed in.
