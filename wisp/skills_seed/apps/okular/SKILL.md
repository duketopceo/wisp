# okular

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Top: menu bar and toolbar. Left: a strip of tabs for Thumbnails, Contents, Reviews and Bookmarks. F7 toggles that panel.
- Annotation tools live under Tools, then Review.

### First steps
- `Ctrl+F` finds text. `Ctrl+G` goes to a page. `Ctrl+Plus` and `Ctrl+Minus` zoom. `Ctrl+P` prints.
- Tools menu, then Review, shows the annotation toolbar: highlight, underline, note and drawing tools.

### Linux pitfalls
- Annotations are kept in Okular's own data unless saved into the file; use File, then Save As to share them.
