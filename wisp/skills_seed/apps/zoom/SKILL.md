# zoom

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Home screen: New meeting, Join, Schedule and Share screen tiles. In a meeting: the toolbar at the bottom with Mute, Video, Share and Chat.
- Settings are behind the gear at the top right of the home screen.

### First steps
- Join: press Join and enter the meeting ID or paste the link.
- The microphone button at the bottom left mutes; `Alt+A` toggles it.
- Share Screen in the toolbar then choose a window or the whole screen.
- Chat opens from the toolbar and shows messages in a side panel.

### Pitfalls
- On Wayland, sharing a screen goes through a portal dialog; the learner chooses the screen there.
