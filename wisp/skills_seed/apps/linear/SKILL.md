# linear

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left sidebar: Inbox, My Issues, then your teams with their Issues, Cycles and Projects. Top right of a list: Filter and Display options.
- Issue pages show status, assignee, priority and labels in the right panel.

### First steps
- The command menu (`Ctrl+K`) finds any action or issue by typing.
- `C` opens a new issue when keyboard shortcuts are available on the page.
- Filter in the list header narrows by status, assignee or label.

### Pitfalls
- Status changes move an issue between board columns; confirm the team before creating an issue.
