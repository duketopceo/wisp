# google-search

## Teaching

### Where things live
- The search box is in the middle of the home page and at the top of results. Under it: All, Images, Videos, News and More, with Tools at the right of that row.
- Settings is at the bottom right of the home page.

### First steps
- Pick Images in the row under the search box for picture results.
- Tools, then Any time, limits results by date.
- Put quotation marks around words to search for an exact phrase.

### Pitfalls
- Ads can appear above results; they are marked Sponsored.
