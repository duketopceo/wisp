# darktable

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Two main views: lighttable (browse, rate, export) and darkroom (edit one image). Switch with `L` and `D`.
- Right in the lighttable: the export module. Right in the darkroom: the editing modules.

### First steps
- `1` to `5` set star ratings, `R` rejects an image. `Space` and `Backspace` move to the next and previous image in the darkroom.
- `Ctrl+E` exports the selected images. `Ctrl+Z` undoes. `Tab` hides the side panels.

### Linux pitfalls
- Edits are saved beside the original in a sidecar file; the original image is never changed until you export.
