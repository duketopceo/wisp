# evince

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Top: the header bar with a sidebar toggle, find, and a menu button. Left: the sidebar (thumbnails, outline, annotations).
- Evince and Papers share most chords, so teach them as one app.

### First steps
- `Ctrl+F` finds text. `Ctrl+L` jumps to a page number. `F9` shows or hides the sidebar.
- `Ctrl+Plus`, `Ctrl+Minus` zoom. `F11` is fullscreen, `Ctrl+P` prints.
- `Space` scrolls down a page; `Page Up` and `Page Down` also move by page.

### Linux pitfalls
- Form fields in a PDF can be filled by clicking them; saving a copy is under the menu button, not `Ctrl+S` for every file.
