# wikipedia

## Teaching

### Where things live
- The search box is at the top. On an article: tabs for Article and Talk, then Read, Edit and View history. The Contents list is on the left, and Tools is at the right of the page.
- Other languages are listed in the language button near the article title.

### First steps
- Type in the top search box and press Enter.
- The language button near the title lists the same article in other languages.
- View history at the top of an article lists past edits.

### Pitfalls
- Editing is open to everyone; point out the citation links rather than treating any single line as final.
