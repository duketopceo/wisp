# konsole

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Top: the menu bar (File, Edit, View, Bookmarks, Settings, Help) over a tab bar. `Ctrl+Shift+M` shows or hides the menu bar.
- Profiles live under Settings, then Edit Current Profile; Settings, then Configure Konsole, covers the rest.

### First steps
- Copy is `Ctrl+Shift+C`, paste is `Ctrl+Shift+V`. Bare `Ctrl+C` stops the running program.
- `Ctrl+Shift+T` new tab, `Ctrl+Shift+N` new window.
- `Ctrl+Shift+F` shows the search bar. The View menu has Split View, for two shells side by side.
- Colours, font and scrollback are in Edit Current Profile, under Appearance and Scrolling.

### Linux pitfalls
- If the menu bar is hidden, the hamburger button or `Ctrl+Shift+M` brings it back; do not say the setting is missing.
