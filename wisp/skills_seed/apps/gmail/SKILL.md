# gmail

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left: Compose, then Inbox and labels. Top: the search box with a filter button. Top right: the settings gear, then See all settings.
- Selecting a message shows archive, delete and label buttons above the list.

### First steps
- Compose starts a message; `C` also does it when keyboard shortcuts are enabled.
- The top search box accepts operators such as from, to and has attachment.
- The Labels area in the left sidebar has a plus to create one.
- Settings, Filters and Blocked Addresses creates filters.

### Pitfalls
- Keyboard shortcuts are off until turned on under See all settings, General. Never read message bodies aloud.
