# google-calendar

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Top: Today, the date arrows, the view switcher (Day, Week, Month) and the settings gear. Left: Create, a mini month, and the calendar list.
- Clicking an empty slot starts an event.

### First steps
- Create button, or click a time slot, opens a new event; add a title and time.
- The view switcher at the top right picks Day, Week or Month.
- In the event editor, the guests field takes email addresses to invite.
- `C` creates an event and `?` lists shortcuts when shortcuts are enabled.

### Pitfalls
- Time zones are set in settings; events made in another zone display in yours.
