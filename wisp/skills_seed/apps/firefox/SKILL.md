# firefox

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Top row: tab strip, then the toolbar with back, forward, reload, the address bar, and the three-line menu at the far right.
- Settings live at `about:preferences`; add-ons at `about:addons`.

### First steps
- `Ctrl+L` or `Alt+D` focuses the address bar. `Ctrl+T` new tab, `Ctrl+W` close, `Ctrl+Shift+T` reopen.
- `Ctrl+Shift+A` opens add-ons. `Ctrl+Shift+I` or `F12` opens DevTools. `F9` toggles Reader View on pages that support it.
