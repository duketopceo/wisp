# clickup

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left sidebar: Home, Inbox, then Spaces with their folders and lists. View tabs such as List, Board and Calendar run along the top of a list.
- The Add Task button is at the top right of a list.

### First steps
- Add Task opens a new task row; type a name and press Enter.
- The plus after the view tabs adds another view.
- Inside a task, the Dates field sets a due date.

### Pitfalls
- The sidebar nesting is Space, Folder, List; a task lives in a list.
