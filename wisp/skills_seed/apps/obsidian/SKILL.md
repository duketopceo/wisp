# obsidian

Obsidian vault navigation and shortcuts:
- **Quick switcher (open note):** `Ctrl+O`
- **Command palette:** `Ctrl+P`
- **Search across vault:** `Ctrl+Shift+F`
- **Toggle edit/reading view:** `Ctrl+E`; **Graph view:** `Ctrl+G`
- **Settings:** `Ctrl+,` (bottom-left gear icon)
