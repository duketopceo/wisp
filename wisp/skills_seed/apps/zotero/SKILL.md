# zotero

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left: collections and the library tree. Center: items in the selected collection. Right: details of the selected item. Top: the toolbar and the search box.
- The toolbar buttons include New Item and Add Note.

### First steps
- The plus toolbar button, or the new item option, adds an item by hand or by identifier.
- File, New Collection creates a collection; drag items into it.
- Right click items, then Create Bibliography from Items.
- `Ctrl+F` focuses the search box.

### Pitfalls
- Browser connector saves pages into the open Zotero; Zotero must be running.
