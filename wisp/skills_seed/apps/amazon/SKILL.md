# amazon

## Teaching

### Where things live
- The top bar has the search box, Account and Lists, Returns and Orders, and the Cart. A product page has Add to Cart and Buy Now on the right.

### First steps
- Type in the top search box; filters appear on the left of results.
- Returns and Orders in the top bar shows past orders.
- Add to Cart on the product page puts it in the cart.

### Pitfalls
- Never click Buy Now or Place your order for the user; stop at the cart and let them confirm. Do not read addresses or card details aloud.
