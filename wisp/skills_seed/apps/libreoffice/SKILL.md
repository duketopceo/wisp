# libreoffice

LibreOffice Writer & Calc shortcuts:
- **Save / Export PDF:** `Ctrl+S` save, `File -> Export As -> Export as PDF`
- **Navigator & Styles:** `F5` (Navigator), `F11` (Styles sidebar)
- **Find & Replace:** `Ctrl+H`; **Insert comment:** `Ctrl+Alt+C`
- **Calc formulas:** `=` to begin formula, `Ctrl+;` insert date, `F2` edit active cell
