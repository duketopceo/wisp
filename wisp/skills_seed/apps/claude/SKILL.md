# claude

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left sidebar: New chat, Chats, Projects, then recents. The message box is at the bottom, with the model picker beside it. Your account menu with Settings is at the bottom left.

### First steps
- New chat at the top of the sidebar starts a fresh conversation.
- Chats in the sidebar lists and searches past conversations.
- Projects groups chats and files around one topic.

### Pitfalls
- Chat history is personal; do not read it aloud.
