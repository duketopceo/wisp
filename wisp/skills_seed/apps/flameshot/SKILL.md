# flameshot

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Flameshot has no main window. It sits in the tray and a capture opens a full-screen selection with a tool bar beside it.
- Settings open from the tray icon menu, under Configuration.

### First steps
- Drag to select a region. The tool bar offers pen, line, arrow, rectangle, circle, marker, text and pixelate.
- `Ctrl+C` or `Enter` copies the capture, `Ctrl+S` saves it, `Ctrl+Z` undoes a mark, `Esc` cancels.

### Linux pitfalls
- On Wayland, capture goes through the desktop portal and may ask for permission first.
