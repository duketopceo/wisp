# krita

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left: the toolbox. Top: the menu bar and the brush toolbar. Right: dockers for layers, colour and brush presets.
- More dockers are under Settings, then Dockers.

### First steps
- `B` selects the freehand brush, `E` toggles the eraser. `Ctrl+Z` undoes. `Ctrl+N` starts a new image.
- `[` and `]` shrink and grow the brush. `Space` with a drag pans the canvas.
- `Tab` hides or shows the dockers. `Insert` adds a new paint layer.

### Linux pitfalls
- `Ctrl+S` saves a `.kra` file that keeps layers; flat images for sharing come from File, then Export.
