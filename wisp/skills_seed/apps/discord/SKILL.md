# discord

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Far left: the server list. Next column: the channels of the selected server. Center: the chat. Bottom left: your name with mute, deafen and the settings gear.
- Right: the member list.

### First steps
- `Ctrl+K` opens the quick switcher to jump to a channel or person.
- Click a voice channel's name to join; the bottom left panel shows the call controls.
- Right click a server icon, then Mute Server.
- The gear next to your name opens User Settings.

### Pitfalls
- Many options sit in per-server settings and in per-channel settings; check which one the learner needs.
