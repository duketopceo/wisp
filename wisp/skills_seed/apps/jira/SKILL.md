# jira

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Top bar: the Create button and a search box. Project pages have a left sidebar with Summary, Board, Backlog and Timeline.
- Opening an issue shows details on the right and comments below.

### First steps
- Click Create in the top bar and pick the project and issue type.
- The Board is columns by status; drag a card to change its status.
- Search in the top bar, or Filters, then View all issues, for saved queries.

### Pitfalls
- Menus differ by Jira plan and by project type; point at what is visible rather than assuming a layout.
