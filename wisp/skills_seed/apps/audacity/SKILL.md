# audacity

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Top: the menu bar and transport buttons (play, record, stop). Centre: the tracks. Bottom: selection and time readouts.

### First steps
- `R` starts recording and `Space` plays or stops. Drag across a track to select a region.
- `Ctrl+X` cuts, `Ctrl+C` copies, `Ctrl+V` pastes, `Ctrl+Z` undoes. `Ctrl+I` splits at the cursor.
- `Ctrl+1` and `Ctrl+3` zoom in and out. Effects are in the Effect menu.

### Linux pitfalls
- `Ctrl+S` saves an Audacity project, not an audio file. Sharing a sound means File, then Export Audio.
