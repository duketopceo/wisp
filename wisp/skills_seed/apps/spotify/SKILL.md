# spotify

Spotify desktop controls:
- **Play / Pause:** `Space`; **Next / Previous track:** `Ctrl+Right` / `Ctrl+Left`
- **Search:** `Ctrl+L` or `Ctrl+K` focuses top search bar
- **Volume:** `Ctrl+Up` / `Ctrl+Down`; **Mute:** `Ctrl+Shift+Down`
- **Queue & devices:** bottom-right toolbar icons next to volume slider
