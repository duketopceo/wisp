# google-meet

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Bottom: the call controls bar with microphone, camera, captions, hand and present. Right side: panels for people and chat. Before joining: a preview with the same toggles.
- More options sits at the right end of the bottom bar.

### First steps
- `Ctrl+D` toggles the microphone and `Ctrl+E` toggles the camera.
- The Present now button in the bottom bar shares a tab, window or screen.
- The CC button toggles captions.
- Chat opens from the right panel buttons.

### Pitfalls
- The browser asks for microphone and camera permission the first time; the lock icon by the address bar re-opens it.
