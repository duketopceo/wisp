# youtube

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Top: the search box. Left: Home, Shorts, Subscriptions and You. Under a video: Like, Share and Save. Player controls are along the bottom of the video.
- The gear in the player holds Playback speed and Quality.

### First steps
- Player keys: `K` play or pause, `J` and `L` skip 10 seconds, `F` fullscreen, `M` mute, `C` captions.
- The CC button in the player toggles captions.
- Save under the video adds it to Watch later or a playlist.

### Pitfalls
- Never click through to ads; skip ads only with the Skip button the page shows.
