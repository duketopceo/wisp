# airtable

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Table tabs run along the top of a base. The left side lists views; the toolbar above the grid holds Filter, Group, Sort and Hide fields.
- The plus at the right end of the column headers adds a field.

### First steps
- Add a record with the plus under the last row, or the Expand icon beside a row.
- Add a field with the plus at the end of the column headers and choose its type.
- Filter in the view toolbar narrows the visible records.

### Pitfalls
- Filters and sorts belong to the current view and change what everyone sees on a shared view.
