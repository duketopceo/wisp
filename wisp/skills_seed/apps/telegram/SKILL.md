# telegram

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left: the chat list with a search box at the top. Center: the open chat. The three line menu at the top left opens settings and Saved Messages.
- Chat details open from the chat header.

### First steps
- `Ctrl+F` searches inside the open chat; the top left box searches everything.
- Menu, Settings, Folders creates folders to group chats.
- The menu lists Saved Messages, a private chat to yourself.
- Attach files with the paperclip icon beside the message box.

### Pitfalls
- Messages are private; teach controls and locations only, without reading conversation text aloud.
