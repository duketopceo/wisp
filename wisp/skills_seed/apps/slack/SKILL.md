# slack

## Teaching

### Where things live
- Left: the sidebar with channels and direct messages. Top: the search bar. Bottom of a channel: the message box with formatting and attach buttons.
- Your profile picture at the bottom left opens status and preferences.

### First steps
- `Ctrl+K` opens the quick switcher for channels and people.
- Hover a message and use the reply in thread icon to start a thread.
- `Ctrl+F` searches; the top search bar also filters by channel or person.
- Click your profile picture, then Set a status.

### Pitfalls
- Workspace and channel menus differ between workspaces; name the area rather than an exact item when unsure.
