## Choreography

- **Listen:** a browser workspace sits behind an ember companion. Choosing
  an example changes its state and identifies the target browser.
- **Act:** scrolling through the desktop separates its window layers in
  depth and moves the ember along a visible target path. This explains
  screen context, browser targeting, and feedback rather than claiming speed.
- **Pause:** the sensitive-action example stops at a human confirmation.
  Only “Approve example” advances it; cancel and replacement turns invalidate
  old timers. No account or microphone is accessed.

One persistent renderer and animation loop. Clamp device pixel ratio to 1.5.
Stop frames offscreen, when hidden, on WebGL loss, and for reduced motion.
All narrative and controls remain usable with the static image or no WebGL.
Reduced motion uses the static companion image and no continuous animation.
